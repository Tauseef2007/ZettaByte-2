Complete Digital Journey

A step-by-step roadmap showing the progression of the ZettaByte campaign launch.

1. Brand Idea

Defining identity, positioning high-performance storage products for heavy multitasking and gaming.

2. Website Development

Designing a high-converting landing page to introduce ZettaByte's unique value proposition.

3. SEO Strategy

Keyword research and technical setup focused on search intent and organic visibility growth.

4. Content Planning

Structuring engagement-driven calendars to establish audience trust and consistency.

5. Social Media Marketing

Executing visual campaigns on Instagram to foster awareness and active community connection.

6. Google Ads

Capturing high-intent customer search queries via highly targeted search advertising.

7. Meta Ads

Scaling targeted reach through visually engaging Facebook and Instagram advertising.

8. Email Marketing

Developing automated retention flows and direct campaign communications via MailChimp.
